## Activity 2 — Eat Better Brand Studio (Module 2; chapters 2.1 Brand Identity, 2.2 Digital Marketing & Community)
Role: member of Eat Better's branding & digital marketing team. 3D creative studio: packaging design workstation, product photography set, large creative displays, colour & typography panels, social campaign screens, marketing performance dashboard, product display shelves — a real agency/brand studio feel.
- Stage 1 Understand the brand: name & tagline, brand identity, Familiar Taste / Better Ingredients / Honest Processes (eatbetterco.com "Our story" cards — photos in `public/brand/library/site/story-*.webp`), colour psychology, consumer perception; inspect real products & packaging.
- Stage 2 Packaging design challenge: pick a category (dry-fruit laddoos, millet namkeen, nuts & mixes, snack combos); interactive packaging editor (colours, typography, product name, product image position, short description, ingredient messaging, front-of-pack arrangement) with a realistic 3D pack mockup that updates live. Student designs are labelled as student-created, never as official packaging.
- Stage 3 Social media creative: Instagram post canvas (image placement, text, headline, CTA, background/layout); teach communicating benefits without unsupported health claims (live claim checker).
- Stage 4 Meta Ads simulation: audience, objective, daily budget, duration, creative, message → simulated impressions, clicks, CTR, engagement, conversions, spend, CPC/CPA with explanations of each metric and how changes affect results. All labelled simulated.
- Final: launch within a fixed budget, evaluate, justify audience + creative.
- Output: PDF "Eat Better Co × SkilliZee — Brand Identity & Marketing Campaign Kit" (student details, brand analysis, target audience profile, positioning, packaging preview, social creative, Meta Ads plan, budget allocation, simulated performance, reflection) + packaging mockup PNG + Instagram creative PNG, all generated from the student's designs.

## Activity 3 — Eat Better Distribution Command Centre (Module 3; chapter 3.4)
Role: distribution & supply-chain manager. 3D warehouse/distribution centre: racks, cartons, conveyor belts, packing stations, scanning stations, loading docks, delivery vehicles, control screens, a visual map of destinations — detailed and functional.
- Stage 1 the four channels (own D2C website, e-commerce marketplaces, quick commerce, corporate & bulk): delivery, fulfilment, order size, customer experience, objectives (use the 3.4 channel comparison matrix).
- Stage 2 warehouse inventory: inspect incoming stock, organise, track inventory, SKUs, expiry/freshness, allocate to channels.
- Stage 3 channel allocation vs a simulated demand forecast across D2C, marketplace, q-commerce dark stores, corporate; show impacts (over-allocation → shortages elsewhere, under-allocation → missed orders, poor rotation → expiry, wrong packaging → damage).
- Stage 4 dynamic disruptions: festive spike, delayed inbound, dark-store stockout, damaged shipment, courier delay, large corporate gifting order — resolve with available resources.
- Stage 5 final mission: limited inventory, multiple orders with deadlines; plan the best strategy keeping freshness and minimising problems.
- Output: PDF "Eat Better Co × SkilliZee — Distribution & Supply Chain Strategy Report" (student details, inventory allocation, channel plan, warehouse decisions, fulfilment strategy, disruption decisions, stockout & wastage analysis, final performance summary, reflection) with actual simulation figures, simulated data labelled.
