## Chapter 4.1: Pricing & Financial Performance

**Objective**
To analyze how D2C food brands structure product pricing, evaluate gross margins and Average Order Value (AOV), and interpret financial performance through reported revenue expansion and profit/loss trends.

**1. Product Pricing & Price-per-Gram Metrics**
Product pricing across FMCG categories varies based on ingredient quality, processing techniques, and packaging formats. Comparing products on a price-per-gram basis reveals how premium positioning and raw material costs influence retail strategy:

| Product SKU | Package Weight | Retail Price (INR) | Price per Gram |
|---|---|---|---|
| Assorted Laddoo Box (24 Laddoos) | 240 g | ₹349 | ~₹1.45 / g |
| Assorted Laddoo Box (42 Laddoos) | 420 g | ₹649 | ~₹1.55 / g |
| Ultimate Snack Box (12 Packs) | 660 g | ₹749 | ~₹1.13 / g |
| Assorted Namkeen Combo (4 Packs) | 400 g | ₹269 | ~₹0.67 / g |

**2. Drivers of Price Variation**
- Raw Material Bill of Materials (BOM): Premium ingredients like dates, almonds, cashews, quinoa, and seeds carry a substantially higher unit cost than refined flour (maida), potatoes, or palm oil used in conventional snacks.
- Clean-Label Value Proposition: Eliminating artificial preservatives, bad fats, and refined sugars commands a value-based pricing premium from health-conscious consumers.
- Volume & Multi-Pack Discounting: Larger combo boxes (e.g., Ultimate Snack Box at ₹1.13/g) reduce shipping and primary packaging overhead per unit, allowing brands to offer lower price-per-gram rates on bundled purchases.
